This repo contains the necessary files to replicate the findings of the network meta-analysis of mortality in COPD patients by GOLD grade, published in Scientific reports in 2026: Niedermaier, T., Mansmann, U. & Holle, R. Estimation of mortality hazard ratios by COPD grade using a systematic review and network meta-analysis: deriving grade-, sex-, and age-specific mortality for a German COPD decision model. Sci Rep 16, 28241 (2026). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-68641-0
- Data of included studies were extracted in Niedermaier_et_al_NMA_data.xlsx.
- Mortality data for Germany and Austria for the general population, stratified by age group and sex, are provided in the Excel sheets.
- Niedermaier_et_al_analysis.R contains the analysis codes.
- study_protocol.pdf includes a study protocol which was published on Open Science Framework (OSF).
